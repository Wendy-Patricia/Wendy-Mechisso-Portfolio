Web Development / Operations Management

PortailOps

An operations management platform for ANETSYS, bringing together customer requests, ticket tracking, and collaboration across internal teams.

Laravel 11 Vue.js Inertia.js MySQL Spatie

Case study

Full-stack web application

ANETSYS · 4 weeks

Overview

PortailOps is a web platform designed to centralize communication between ANETSYS customers and teams. Built over four weeks, the MVP covers authentication, ticket management, subscriptions and offers, as well as tools for support, sales, and administration teams.

Context and goals

The project aimed to replace several manual processes with a single workspace, giving customers transparent updates on their requests and teams a clear view of tickets and workload. The scope and development plan were defined with my internship supervisor, then refined using team feedback and the company’s real-world service catalog.

My contribution

I developed the full-stack application independently under the supervision of my internship supervisor. I set up the architecture, roles, and customer and internal workflows, then added ANETSYS’s service catalog and official pricing based on documents provided by the company.

Development over four weeks

Week 1 - Foundations: initialized Laravel 11, configured MySQL, and integrated Laravel Breeze, Vue.js, and Inertia.js. I defined four roles - administrator, technical support, sales, and customer - with Spatie Permission, protected routes by role, and set up the Git repository with main and develop branches.

Week 2 - Customer portal: built a personalized dashboard, a ticket system categorized by service and request type, file attachments, and customer-team comments. Web incidents are classified as critical, major, or minor, with contractual resolution times shown; sales requests have dedicated request types. Customers can also view their subscriptions and available offers.

Week 3 - Back office: built a filterable support ticket list and a Kanban board, with ticket details, assignment, status and priority management, customer replies, and internal notes. The sales workspace handles sales requests and manages offers, services, and subscriptions. Administrators have global access to tickets, accounts, and activity logs. Only administrators can create accounts; new users receive a temporary password to replace on their first sign-in.

Week 4 - Consolidation: added in-app and email notifications for ticket creation or assignment, status changes, and new comments. A notification bell with a counter appears in the navigation bar. I also created the public landing page and custom error pages, and ran end-to-end tests across user flows.

Technical approach

The application uses Laravel 11, Vue.js, and Inertia.js to connect controllers to reactive views without a separate REST API. MySQL stores data in a relational database of 22 tables covering users, tickets, comments, attachments, subscriptions, offers, invoices, notifications, and logs. Spatie Permission manages roles, while Spatie Activity Log records important actions.

Challenges

The initial MySQL migrations failed with “Specified key was too long.” I resolved this by setting a 255-character default limit for indexed columns in AppServiceProvider and applying the same fix to the Spatie Permission migrations. Learning Vue.js, resolving Inertia component and prop issues, and fixing missing Eloquent relationships required rapid learning and iterative debugging in the browser console.

Results and impact

The delivered MVP includes customer, support, sales, and administrator workspaces, ticket and offer management, automatic notifications, and timestamped logs attributed to the user who performed each action. One-click assignment and automatic resolution time calculations reduce manual work; centralization gives teams better visibility and improves communication between sales and technical departments.

The platform is deployed and used by ANETSYS. The project also includes partial GDPR measures, including consent and complete data deletion on request, and is hosted in France by OVH.

What I learned

This internship gave me the opportunity to deliver a full-stack project from start to finish, quickly improve my skills in Vue.js and Inertia.js, and deepen my understanding of role management, Eloquent relationships, notifications, and audit trails. Working with the team also taught me to evolve features in response to concrete business needs.
