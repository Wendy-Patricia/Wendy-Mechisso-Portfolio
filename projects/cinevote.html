Cinema / Web Application

Overview

CineVote is a cinema-focused web application for browsing films, actors, and directors. Registered users can take part as electors or candidates, while administrators review community-submitted content.

Context

The project brings together a public cinema catalogue and a community-driven participation workflow. Its access is organized around distinct roles for visitors, electors, candidates, and administrators.

Problem

A community catalogue needs a way to accept new film and people-related entries without making every submission immediately public. Voting also needs clear eligibility rules and a defined period before results are shown.

Technical approach

The application uses Symfony 7 with PHP, Doctrine ORM, and Twig. Controllers handle catalogue, proposal, and voting requests; Doctrine entities model users, films, actors, directors, categories, and votes. Role-based access distinguishes electors, candidates, and administrators.

Candidate submissions are stored as pending until an administrator approves or rejects them. Electors can vote once in each of three categories: films, actors, and directors. Results become available after the configured voting end date.

Challenges

The implementation has to coordinate permissions with content moderation: candidates can submit entries, but only validated entries appear in the public catalogue. Voting adds another set of rules, including preventing repeat votes per category and withholding results until the voting period has ended.
