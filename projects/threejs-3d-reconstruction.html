3D / WebGL

Overview

Scène Maritime is an interactive 3D project built with Three.js and WebGL. It depicts three sailboats on a reflective ocean beneath a warm sunset, with orbit controls and real-time scene adjustments.

Context

Created for the 3D Modeling course at IUT Saint-Dié during the 2025–2026 academic year, the project was inspired by a watercolor painting of sailboats heading toward a vivid sunset.

Problem

The challenge was to translate the painting's lighting, reflections, atmospheric depth and sense of movement into a real-time 3D scene that viewers could explore and interact with.

Technical approach

The scene combines Three.js components including Sky, Water, Reflector and OrbitControls. A custom GLSL shader distorts the water reflection, while animated cloud sprites and particle-based fog add movement and atmospheric depth.

The boats use textured materials with normal and specular maps. Directional lighting and soft shadow maps create the sunset illumination. A control panel lets viewers move and reset the boats, adjust fog density, and change the opacity of hulls and sails.

Challenges

The project brings together several rendering techniques that need to work in the same scene: the animated water surface, a custom reflective shader, atmospheric fog, shadows and interactive boat movement. Keeping the scene adjustable while preserving the sunset mood was also an important part of the implementation.

Result

The result is an explorable sunset scene featuring three movable sailboats, animated clouds, adjustable fog and boat transparency, soft shadows and a distorted reflection on the water.

What I learned

The project provided practical experience with Three.js scene composition, WebGL materials, texture maps, GLSL shader customization, raycasting and real-time interaction controls.
